Robust, pluralistic and efficient alignment of large language models, with applications to digital humanities

2025 – · Chaire de professeur junior, Université Lumière Lyon 2, funded by the French National Research Agency (ANR)

Large language models are aligned with human preferences through reinforcement learning and preference optimization (RLHF, DPO). My chair studies the decision-making foundations of this step, and its use in the humanities, along four lines:

  • Robustness: keeping preference optimization reliable when feedback is noisy or corrupted, and when deployment data differs from training data.
  • Pluralistic alignment: aligning models with several legitimate, possibly conflicting, preference distributions instead of a single consensus, and letting personalized models learn from each other.
  • Efficiency: reducing the computational and sample cost of reinforcement learning post-training, so that it can run on modest hardware.
  • Digital humanities: using language models as research tools in the humanities and social sciences, where interpretations are plural and sources are noisy, which makes robust and pluralistic alignment essential.

Code: SHIFT-Lab, a framework for studying distribution shifts and lightweight corrections in LLM-based decision pipelines

People: Emma Carpanin (Master's student, 2026); Mohammad Amanlou, Yasaman Amou Jafari, Amir MalekHosseini (Bachelor's students, 2026)

Decision-making in Markov decision processes with imprecise rewards

PhD (2016) and follow-up work

When the rewards of an MDP are only partially known, which policy should we choose, and which questions should we ask the user to learn the rewards that matter? This line of work, started in my PhD, computes policies that minimize the maximum regret and studies when deterministic policies are enough.

Papers: Deterministic policies based on maximum regrets in MDPs with imprecise rewards, AI Communications, 2021 [DOI]

Thesis: Elicitation and planning in Markov decision processes with unknown rewards, 2016

Code: Optimization
