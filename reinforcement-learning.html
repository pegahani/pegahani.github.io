Reinforcement Learning

Course Overview

This course introduces the fundamentals of reinforcement learning, a machine learning paradigm where an agent learns to make optimal decisions by interacting with an environment. Through theory and hands-on projects, students will understand key concepts and implement RL algorithms for real-world applications.

Learning Objectives

  • ▸ Understand the fundamentals of Markov Decision Processes (MDPs)
  • ▸ Implement and compare value-based and policy-based algorithms
  • ▸ Apply RL techniques to optimization and control problems
  • ▸ Analyze robustness and safety in RL systems

Topics Covered

  • ▸ Markov Decision Processes and dynamic programming
  • ▸ Temporal difference learning and Q-learning
  • ▸ Policy gradient methods and actor-critic algorithms
  • ▸ Deep reinforcement learning and neural networks
  • ▸ Multi-agent and offline reinforcement learning
  • ▸ Applications to optimization and control systems
